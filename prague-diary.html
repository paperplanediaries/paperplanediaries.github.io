4:00 AM · Prague

The postcard incident 😂

The taxi driver spoke no English.
I spoke no Czech.

Somehow he handed me a bunch of postcards.

I thought he wanted me to buy one,
so I picked one and tried to ask how much.

He looked at me.

Then he gestured for me to take
the entire stack.

...so I did. 😂
